Wood Post Lay-Down Fence Clip

Makes your wood post fence look smart, and is super easy to install.

The strongest, most versatile solution

Again, the stretch-through design provides the strongest, most versatile fencing solution on the market, with tons of options and stronger-than-wire strength.

With this system, an existing fence can be tightened by stretching wire every ¼ mile rather than between each pair of posts. This saves having to remove staples on wood posts.

Snow loads push steel posts into the ground or bend them when the wire is left installed. Laying the wire down prevents this.

  • Fits 4", 5" or 6" wood posts.
  • Each clip comes with 2 galvanized nails and 1 duplex nail. They're ready to go!
  • For a super-tough installation, use grabber screws (not provided). Take your pick.

Electric top wire

If you need an electric top wire or two, it's a piece of cake to do with our insulator.

Note: in some cases the insulator will restrict stretch-through with barbed wire and require insulator positioning. Use un-barbed wire or tape if this is undesirable.
