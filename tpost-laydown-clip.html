Steel T-Post Lay-Down Fence Clip

Our most popular clip, with its installation tool. Its ease of installation, durability, strength and ease of maintenance make it a real winner.

Stretching is a snap

The stretch-through design of all our clips, with all types of wire, is the best feature. With our clip system an existing fence can be stretched up to ¼ mile or farther, depending on conditions. Forget removing staples or tie wire (on steel posts) to stretch conventional fences.

Steel T-Posts in snow country

Steel T-Posts can now be used in snow areas too, where winter snow loads push the posts into the ground or bend them when the wire is left attached to the post. In winter pasture areas with no animals, the fence can simply be laid down in the fall and put back up in the spring, with a lot less hassle, maintenance and repair, and easy stretching.

This feature alone will pay for the clips in labor in just a few seasons, and give you time for other farming responsibilities or leisure.

Steel T-Posts are normally less expensive, last longer and are easier to install, again saving money and time.

Stronger than tie wire

The Steel Post Lay-Down Clip is several times stronger than the tie-wire method, and in most cases the wire will break before the clip. We have found that the wire will break or the T-Post will bend before the clip fails.

If a clip gets bent, an occasional straightening is usually all that's required. If it does get damaged, replacement is a snap!

We've also noticed that with the stretch-through feature, wire breakage is reduced because the wire has its whole length to move and stretch if necessary. The tie-wire method limits movement, and breaks are more frequent.

Like all our other Lay-Down Clips, it can be used with our Super-Heavy Insulator for all types of electric fencing needs.
