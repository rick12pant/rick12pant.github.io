Steel T-Post Permanent Fence Clip

Its ease of installation, durability, strength and ease of maintenance make it a real winner.

The stretch-through design of all our clips, with all types of wire, is the best feature.

Our Steel T-Post Permanent Fencing Clip Solution, fitting all T-Posts, is another of our tough fencing solutions for use with barbed, smooth or hi-tensile wire. We call it our Permanent Fence Clip because in no-snow, no-lay-down areas of the country it's an install-and-forget solution to most fencing situations.

It can be used with all types of wire, and in most cases is indestructible. Although we call it permanent, it can be removed with a little effort using our tool and re-installed if necessary.

You'll find these clips from coast to coast: along highways, on BLM and U.S. Forest Service land, and on private ranches and farms.

Again, the stretch-through design allows for easy stretch maintenance, along with fast and easy installation using our fencing tool. That lowers your maintenance costs and saves you time and money!

No more saggy, loose, dangerous wires here.
